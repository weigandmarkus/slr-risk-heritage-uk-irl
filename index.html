About this Project

Methodology & Uncertainty

Flood zones represent mean projected high-water marks based on global sea-level rise models. This is a "bathtub model" visualization; actual flood extent may vary due to local topography, coastal defenses, and storm surges.
Uncertainty is visualized by comparing the three SSP scenarios below.

Scenario Definitions (IPCC AR6)

  • SSP1-2.6 (Low): The "Paris Agreement" goal. Drastic emissions cuts; warming limited to ~1.8°C.
  • SSP2-4.5 (Medium): "Middle of the Road." Current trends continue; warming ~2.7°C.
  • SSP5-8.5 (High): "Fossil-Fueled Development." Worst-case scenario; warming >4.4°C.

Data Sources

  • Basemap: Stadia Maps, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap.
  • Flood Projections: Aggregated from IPCC AR6 Sea Level Projection Tool.
  • Archaeological Data: Historic England, HES, CADW, National Monuments Service.
