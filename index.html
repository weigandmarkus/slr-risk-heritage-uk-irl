Vienna’s Oldest Trees:
A Look at Pre-1950 Heritage

Data Findings:

Filtering for trees planted before 1950 reveals that the oldest vegetation is concentrated in the parks, while street trees are generally younger.

Additionally, there is a visible correlation between age and trunk size. The oldest trees consistently exhibit the thickest trunk sizes.

Projection: Transverse Mercator
Scale Types: Linear (Color, Size, Stroke)
